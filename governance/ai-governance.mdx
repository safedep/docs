title: AI Governance
description: "Inventory the AI libraries and SDKs your applications use with an AI BOM generated from source code."
---

AI libraries and SDKs get added to application code without central tracking, leaving security and compliance teams without an inventory of what AI services the code actually calls.

An **AI BOM** (AI Bill of Materials) answers that question. `vet` analyzes your source code, detects the AI and LLM SDKs it calls, and produces a [CycloneDX](/concepts/sbom) BOM with file-level and line-level evidence.
